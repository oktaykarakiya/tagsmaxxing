Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

Last updated: June 1, 2026

{# 1. Introduction #}

1. Introduction

This Data Processing Agreement (“DPA”) forms part of the Terms of Service between tagsmaxxing (“Processor”) and the customer (“Controller”). It governs the processing of personal data submitted to the tagsmaxxing service and reflects the parties’ agreement with regard to the processing of personal data in compliance with applicable data protection laws, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

{# 2. Definitions #}

2. Definitions

Terms used in this DPA have the meanings given in the GDPR, unless otherwise defined. “Personal Data,” “Processing,” “Controller,” “Processor,” and “Data Subject” have the meanings set out in Article 4 of the GDPR.

{# 3. Scope & Purpose #}

3. Scope & Purpose of Processing

The Processor shall process Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Controller, including with regard to transfers of personal data to a third country or an international organization, unless required to do so by applicable law. The subject matter and duration of the processing, the nature and purpose, the types of personal data, and the categories of data subjects are set out in Annex 1 of this DPA.

{# 4. Processor Obligations #}

4. Obligations of the Processor

The Processor shall:

  • Process Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Controller;
  • Ensure that persons authorized to process the Personal Data have committed themselves to confidentiality;
  • Implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk (see Section 7);
  • Assist the Controller in fulfilling its obligations regarding data subject rights, data breach notification, and data protection impact assessments;
  • Make available to the Controller all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA;
  • At the Controller’s choice, delete or return all Personal Data upon termination of the service.

5. Sub-Processors

The Processor shall inform the Controller of any intended changes concerning the addition or replacement of sub-processors, giving the Controller the opportunity to object. Sub-processors are contractually bound to data protection obligations no less protective than those in this DPA.

{# 6. International Transfers #}

6. International Data Transfers

The Processor may transfer Personal Data to sub-processors located outside the European Economic Area (EEA) provided that appropriate safeguards are in place, including Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or an adequacy decision, as applicable. The Controller may request copies of the relevant transfer safeguards.

{# 7. Security Measures #}

7. Technical & Organizational Measures

The Processor implements the following measures to protect Personal Data:

Measure Implementation
Encryption at rest AES-256-GCM per-tenant encryption keys; files stored encrypted; key hierarchy with KEK/DEK pattern
Encryption in transit TLS 1.2+ for all connections; HSTS enabled
Tenant isolation PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) enforced via non-superuser app role; per-tenant connection context
Access control Role-based access (Owner/Admin/Member); session-based authentication with Argon2id password hashing; API token auth with SHA-256 hashed storage
Key management Key Encryption Key (KEK) per cluster; Data Encryption Keys (DEKs) per tenant; KEK unwraps DEKs at access time
Data deletion Cryptographic shredding: deleting the DEK renders all encrypted data irrecoverable
Logging & monitoring Structured logging; decrypt-access audit trail; anomaly detection on encryption key usage
{# 8. Data Residency #}

8. Data Residency

Where available, the Controller may select a data residency region. When a region is selected, file storage and database hosting are confined to that geographic region. Data residency is configured at the tenant level and applies to both storage and processing infrastructure.

{# 9. Audit Rights #}

9. Audit Rights

The Controller may, no more than once per calendar year and upon 30 days’ written notice, audit the Processor’s compliance with this DPA. Audits shall be conducted during normal business hours, shall not unreasonably disrupt the Processor’s operations, and shall be at the Controller’s expense. The Processor shall provide reasonable cooperation and access to relevant records.

{# 10. Term & Termination #}

10. Term & Termination

This DPA remains in effect for the duration of the Controller’s use of the Service. Upon termination, the Processor shall, at the Controller’s election, delete or return all Personal Data. The cryptographic shredding process is described in our Data Retention & Erasure policy.

{# 11. Contact #}

11. Data Protection Contact

For questions regarding this DPA or to exercise rights under it, contact our Data Protection Officer at oktaykarakiya@protonmail.com.
