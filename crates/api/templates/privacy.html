Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

{# 1. Overview #}

1. Overview

tagsmaxxing (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the tagsmaxxing file knowledge base service (“the Service”), how we use it, and the choices you have.

{# 2. Information We Collect #}

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your email address, workspace name, and a hashed password. If you sign up for a paid plan, our payment processor (Stripe) collects your payment card details — we do not store full card numbers on our servers.

2.2 Uploaded Content

The files you upload are stored encrypted at rest. We process file content to extract text, generate AI tags and summaries, and create search embeddings. Processing is performed by the AI model providers you configure (local or remote); see our DPA for sub-processor details.

2.3 Usage Data

We collect metadata about your use of the Service: number of files uploaded, storage used, AI model calls (token counts), search queries, and account activity. This data is used for billing, quota enforcement, and service improvement. We do not log the content of your files beyond what is needed to provide the Service.

2.4 Technical Data

Our servers automatically log IP addresses, browser user agents, request timestamps, and HTTP status codes for operational purposes (security monitoring, rate limiting, debugging). These logs are retained for a maximum of 30 days.

{# 3. How We Use Information #}

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service;
  • Process your files (extract text, generate tags and embeddings, enable search);
  • Enforce usage quotas and bill for paid plans;
  • Send service-related communications (account verification, password resets, billing notices, quota warnings);
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents;
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information or uploaded content to third parties.

{# 4. Data Sharing & Sub-Processors #}

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Sub-Processors

We share data with third-party sub-processors only to the extent necessary to provide the Service. These include:

  • Cloud storage — encrypted file storage (e.g. Backblaze B2);
  • Payment processor — Stripe, Inc. for billing and subscription management;
  • Email provider — for sending transactional emails (verification, password reset, notifications);
  • AI model providers — if you use remote AI models, file content is transmitted to the providers you configure. A full list of configured providers is available in our Data Processing Agreement.

Each sub-processor is bound by data processing terms consistent with this policy. We remain responsible for their compliance.

{# 5. Data Retention #}

5. Data Retention

We retain your account information and uploaded content for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all your files, metadata, and personal information are permanently deleted. See our Data Retention & Erasure policy for the cryptographic shredding process.

{# 6. Security #}

6. Security

We implement technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption at rest: All uploaded files are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using per-tenant encryption keys;
  • Encryption in transit: All connections use TLS 1.2 or higher;
  • Tenant isolation: PostgreSQL Row-Level Security ensures strict data separation between tenants;
  • Access control: Role-based access with session management and API token authentication;
  • Password hashing: Argon2id (memory-hard) for password storage.

No system is completely secure. If you discover a vulnerability, please report it to oktaykarakiya@protonmail.com.

{# 7. Your Rights #}

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data;
  • Rectify: Correct inaccurate personal data;
  • Erase: Delete your account and all associated data;
  • Export: Receive your data in a portable format (see the FAQ);
  • Object: Object to certain processing activities.

To exercise these rights, contact us at oktaykarakiya@protonmail.com.

{# 8. Cookies #}

8. Cookies & Tracking

We use essential cookies to provide the Service (session management, CSRF protection). We do not use third-party tracking cookies or analytics services that collect data for advertising. See our Cookie Policy for details.

{# 9. Changes #}

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via email and/or an in-app notice at least 30 days before they take effect. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

{# 10. Contact #}

10. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, contact our Data Protection Officer at oktaykarakiya@protonmail.com.
