Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

{# Overview #}

1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy (“AUP”) sets out the rules for using the Local KB file knowledge base service (“the Service”). By using the Service, you agree to comply with this AUP. Violation of this AUP may result in suspension or termination of your account.

{# Prohibited Content #}

2. Prohibited Content

You may not upload, store, or process content that:

  • Violates any applicable law, regulation, or third-party right;
  • Infringes intellectual property rights (copyright, trademark, patent, trade secret);
  • Contains child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or exploits minors in any way;
  • Depicts or promotes violence, terrorism, or hate speech against individuals or groups;
  • Contains malware, viruses, ransomware, or other harmful code;
  • Constitutes phishing, fraud, or deceptive content designed to defraud others;
  • Violates privacy rights or contains personal information shared without consent;
  • Is used to harass, stalk, intimidate, or threaten others.
{# Prohibited Activities #}

3. Prohibited Activities

You may not:

  • Attempt to gain unauthorized access to the Service, other users’ accounts, or the underlying infrastructure;
  • Probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of the Service without prior written authorization;
  • Interfere with or disrupt the Service, its servers, or networks (e.g., DDoS, excessive API requests);
  • Circumvent any security, authentication, or access control measures;
  • Use the Service to mine cryptocurrency or perform other resource-intensive computations unrelated to file processing;
  • Resell, sublicense, or distribute the Service as a standalone product to third parties;
  • Create multiple free accounts to circumvent quota limits;
  • Reverse engineer, decompile, or extract the source code of the Service, except as permitted by applicable open-source licenses.
{# AI Usage #}

4. AI Model Usage

When using AI model features (tagging, embedding, reranking, captioning), you must not:

  • Submit content designed to exploit, jailbreak, or prompt-inject the models;
  • Use the AI features to generate illegal, harmful, or policy-violating outputs;
  • Exceed the rate limits or token quotas of your plan tier.
{# Enforcement #}

5. Enforcement

We reserve the right to investigate violations of this AUP and take appropriate action, including:

  • Removing or blocking access to prohibited content;
  • Suspending or terminating your account without notice in cases of severe violation;
  • Issuing a warning and requesting corrective action for minor or first-time violations;
  • Reporting illegal content or activity to law enforcement authorities.

We will provide notice of enforcement actions where reasonably possible, unless doing so would compromise an investigation or violate legal obligations.

{# Reporting #}

6. Reporting Violations

If you become aware of any violation of this AUP, please report it to oktaykarakiya@protonmail.com. We review all reports and take appropriate action. We protect the confidentiality of reporters to the extent permitted by law.

{# Changes #}

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this AUP from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via email and/or an in-app notice. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
