The network-service clause

AGPLv3 adds one important condition beyond the standard GPL: if you modify the software and make it available to users interacting with it over a computer network (including as a SaaS or hosted service), you must make your modifications available under the same license. This closes the "network service loophole" that allows companies to take open-source code, improve it privately, and offer it as a proprietary service without giving anything back.
