Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

{# What Are Cookies #}

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and provide information to the site operators. Cookies may be “session” cookies (deleted when you close your browser) or “persistent” cookies (remain until they expire or are deleted).

{# How We Use Cookies #}

2. How We Use Cookies

tagsmaxxing uses only strictly necessary cookies to provide the Service. We do not use cookies for advertising, analytics, or tracking purposes.

Cookie Name Purpose Duration Type
__Host-session Authentication session — identifies your logged-in session so you can access protected areas of the Service 24 hours (sliding) Essential
__Host-csrf Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) protection — prevents malicious sites from submitting forms on your behalf Session Essential
{# Cookie Properties #}

3. Cookie Security Properties

Our cookies are configured with security best practices:

  • HttpOnly: Cookies are not accessible to JavaScript, preventing cross-site scripting (XSS) attacks from stealing session tokens;
  • Secure: Cookies are only transmitted over HTTPS connections (except in local development);
  • SameSite=Lax: Cookies are not sent on cross-site requests, protecting against cross-site request forgery (CSRF);
  • __Host- prefix: The __Host- prefix enforces that the cookie is set with `Secure` and `Path=/` and is never sent to subdomains.
{# Third-Party Cookies #}

4. Third-Party Cookies

tagsmaxxing does not set any third-party cookies. We do not use tracking pixels, social media widgets, analytics beacons, or advertising networks. The only third-party domain that may set cookies is Stripe, if you interact with Stripe’s embedded checkout or customer portal — those cookies are governed by Stripe’s Cookie Policy.

{# Managing Cookies #}

5. Managing & Disabling Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to manage or disable cookies through their settings. However, because our cookies are strictly necessary for the Service to function (session management, CSRF protection), disabling them will prevent you from logging in or using the Service.

For guidance on managing cookies in your browser, refer to your browser’s documentation:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
